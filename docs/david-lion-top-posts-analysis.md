## What These Posts Actually Do Differently

### 1. Comment triggers gating real value (Posts 1, 2, 4)

Three of your five top posts park value behind a single word in the
comments:

- *"Comment 'Framework' and I'll send it over to you!"* → 84 comments
- *"Drop the word Reset in the comments and I'll DM you the link"* → 57 comments
- *"Drop VISION below or DM me for Details!"* → workshop CTA

This is by far your strongest mechanism. On the Reset post, you can
literally watch it work in the raw data — "Reset", "Reset", "Reset",
one after another. Friction is zero, so people comment on autopilot.

### 2. You reply to literally every comment, individually

On the birthday post (#2), **30+ of the 57 comments are your own
replies** — every person addressed by name, short, warm: *"thank you
lion sister <3"*, *"check DM :)"*, *"DM <3"*. That doubles the comment
count and pings the algorithm twice — once on the original, once on
the reply notification. The Meta algorithm reads this as "live
conversation" and pushes the post further.

You do this on top posts. The question is whether you also do it on
median posts. If not, that alone is probably worth a chunk of reach.

### 3. The hook lands before "see more" cuts the post off

- **Post 1**: bold Unicode headline that nails your exact positioning
  ("𝐆𝐨-𝐓𝐨 𝐒𝐨𝐦𝐚𝐭𝐢𝐜 𝐁𝐮𝐬𝐢𝐧𝐞𝐬𝐬 𝐂𝐨𝐚𝐜𝐡 𝐟𝐨𝐫 𝐒𝐩𝐢𝐫𝐢𝐭𝐮𝐚𝐥 𝐄𝐧𝐭𝐫𝐞𝐩𝐫𝐞𝐧𝐞𝐮𝐫𝐬")
- **Post 4**: raw vulnerability up top — *"Last year, I launched a
  membership that not a single person bought."*
- **Post 3**: mini-drama — *"Gonna purge my friends list soon."*

All three stop the scroll in the first two lines. Your median post
probably doesn't.

### 4. Specific numbers, not vague claims

Post 1 alone lists **four named client wins with hard numbers**:
Florian 50€ → 1800€, Robin 6k+ launch, Sana selling her 2.4k 1:1
offer, Jan booked weeks ahead in Vienna.

Post 4 cites €15,000 generated from "failed" launches and a

That kind of specificity is rare in the coaching space, and it does
two things at once: builds credibility and gives the algorithm

### 5. Identity statements as pattern interrupts

Across your top posts:

- *"I am not a hustler."*
- *"I'm not a coach that hypes you up. I'm not 'high vibe'.
  I don't motivate you."* (your Jan 7 post, score 140)
- *"Can we please stop idolizing Slowness?"* (Dec 11, score 101)

You're polarizing *against* genre clichés, not against people. That
filters in your actual audience and filters out the noise. It also
gives you a distinct voice in a feed full of beige coaches.

### 6. Two formats win — for different reasons

**Long-form wisdom essays** (Posts 1, 4): high *likes* + shares.
Posts like #4 give readers a quotable aphorism they can co-sign:
*"Perspective is not a cheap mindset trick. It's an expression of
leadership."* People silently agree and hit like.

**Short relational / transactional posts** (Posts 2, 3, 5): high
*comment* counts. The reply can be a single emoji, so volume goes up
hard.

Both work. Mixing them is more powerful than picking one, because
the algorithm rewards different signals.

### 7. Vulnerability in 4 of the 5 top posts

Income swings (#1), turning 32 (#2), love note to Katharina (#5),
flopped launches (#4). Only the friends-list purge is purely
transactional. Vulnerability is your actual edge — it fits the somatic
coach brand and it builds para-social bond. People don't engage with
expertise. They engage with *people who happen to have expertise*.

## Four Concrete Levers, in Priority Order

**1. One comment-trigger post per week.**
Real value behind it — mini-training, checklist, workshop link, audit.
Don't inflate it; once the list catches on that *every* post is gated,
the trick dulls. One per week is the sweet spot.

**2. Reply discipline on every post.**
By name, 3–10 words, within 24h. You do this on the breakout posts —
the question is whether you do it on the quiet ones. If not, that's
free reach you're leaving on the table.

**3. Harder first three lines.**
Either bold headline + identity claim, or raw vulnerability statement,
or pattern interrupt ("Stop doing X"). If the first line is context
or setup, rewrite it. The hook should be the *conclusion* of the
post, dropped at the top.

**4. Format rotation, consciously.**
Aim for a weekly mix:
- 1× long-form wisdom essay (the Post #4 shape)
- 2× short relational / question posts (the Post #3 / #5 shape)
- 1× comment-trigger with value
- Avoid: 5× medium-length coach content. That's the dead zone.
